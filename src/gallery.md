## 📄 Starters

Three templates to copy: type in any of them and it becomes your own deck.

| Template | Open from GitHub |
|---|---|
| **Blank**: a first card, a section and a detail | [blank.md →](/gh/slidetake/slidetake/examples/blank.md) |
| **Talk**: why it matters, the idea, trade-offs, summary | [talk.md →](/gh/slidetake/slidetake/examples/talk.md) |

## 🌐 On your own site

[`examples/embed.html`](https://github.com/slidetake/slidetake/blob/main/examples/embed.html) is a whole page hosting a deck with `slidetake.js`: the deck, your site's trail and colours, and one script tag.

## ➕ Your own

Put a deck in any GitHub repository and open it here:

```text
slidetake.com/gh/you/repo/talks/deck.md
slidetake.com/gh/you/repo@branch/deck.md
```

Or keep it on your computer and run `npx slidetake deck.md`.
