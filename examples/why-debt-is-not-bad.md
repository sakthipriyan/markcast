## 1️⃣ When to Take Debt ✅ {#when-to-take-debt}

| Purpose | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| **Home Loan** | Asset creation + low interest |
| **Vehicle Loan** | For genuine need + within budget |
| **Education Loan** | Future earning potential |

### Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

> ⚠️ **Must have BEFORE taking any debt:**

- Emergency fund in place (6-12 months of expenses)
- Insurance coverage (health, term, accidental)
- Stable income & job security
- EMI should be much lower than income (ideally <40%)

## 2️⃣ When NOT to Take Debt ❌ {#when-not-to-take-debt}

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| **Debt for Investment** | Market risk + Interest burden = Double trouble |
| **Consumer Durables** | Depreciating assets, not wealth building |
| **Lifestyle Expenses** | Vacations, weddings, gadgets on EMI |
| **Credit Card Debt** | 30-48% interest rates will destroy wealth |
| **Peer Pressure** | Social status purchases you can't afford |

### Red Flags {#red-flags}

- Unstable income
- No emergency fund or insurance coverage
- Already high EMI burden
- Using debt to fund lifestyle

## 3️⃣ Arbitrage Opportunity 💰 {#arbitrage}

> **Arbitrage** is
> portfolio growth > interest on debt

### Example {#arbitrage-example}

| Scenario | Details |
|---|---|
| Loan Interest | 9% per year |
| Portfolio Returns | 14% per year |
| Arbitrage Gain | 5% per year |

- Keep money invested instead of prepaying low-interest loans
- Your money grows faster than loan interest accumulates

## 4️⃣ Personal Experience 🔄 {#personal-experience}

<!-- camera: 40% flip -->

> **I treated debt as the enemy**

- Originated from exposure to non-institutional loans (2-3% per month)
- Foreclosed home loan early rather than letting it run its course
- Liquidated investments to buy a vehicle instead of taking a loan
- Avoided credit cards for a long time

**Result: Lost opportunity for better wealth growth**

### Current Approach {#current-approach}

> **Loan interest is a speed breaker.
> Liquidation is a dead end.**

- With Emergency Fund & Insurance in place
- Multi-asset global portfolio growing faster than typical loan rates
- More comfortable with strategic debt now
- Future vehicle purchases: Considering loans over liquidation
- Using credit cards responsibly for rewards

**Outcome: More capital allocated towards portfolio growth**

## 5️⃣ Debt Decision Framework 🧠 {#framework}

<!-- camera: on -->

> **Cultural conditioning:**
> "Debt is bad, avoid at all costs"

**Reality Check**

- Not all debt is bad debt
- Strategic debt can accelerate wealth building
- Completely avoiding debt may mean missing opportunities
- Focus: good debt vs bad debt, not debt vs no debt
  - **Good Debt:** Creates assets, low interest rates, manageable EMIs
  - **Bad Debt:** Funds consumption, high interest rates, stretched budget

### Build vs Liquidate vs Loan {#build-liquidate-loan}

| Option | When to Choose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Build** | Long-term goal and time available | Down payment in 3 years |
| **Liquidate** | Short-term need and high loan rates | Personal loan >15% |
| **Loan** | Urgent need or low rates or good ROI | Home loan at 8% |

### Emotional vs Financial {#emotional-vs-financial}

| Decision Type | Choose When | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Emotional** | Peace of mind > Returns | Prepaying low-interest home loan for comfort |
| **Financial** | Returns > Emotional comfort | Keeping loan, investing surplus |

**Both Are Valid**

- Financial optimization isn't everything
- Mental peace has value
- Your money, your rules

> 💡 **Know the numbers, then commit**

## 6️⃣ Summary 📝 {#summary}

- 💰 Debt is a tool, not an enemy
- 🛡️ Emergency fund + insurance first
- ✅ Good debt creates assets
- ❌ Bad debt funds consumption
- 📊 Arbitrage: Invest surplus vs prepay
- 🧠 Know numbers, then commit
